  written in your own hw1.js, [-3, -2, 0, 1, 4] expecting 3, fails because the
  function returns 2.

  The bug: In JavaScript, -3 % 2 is -1, not 1. So x[i] % 2 === 1 misses negative
  odd numbers. Changing it to x[i] % 2 !== 0 (or Math.abs(x[i] % 2) === 1)
  would fix it. I didn't change hw1.js, since this looks like the bug the
